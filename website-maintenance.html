Maintenance & support

Website maintenance and support

Practical help with the website you already have, whether we built it or not. Updates, fixes, and someone to explain the technical side in plain language.

Ask about support → What we can help with

What we can help with

The everyday jobs that keep a site useful

Content updates

New hours, prices, services, photos or staff. Send us the change and we’ll make it, or show you how to do it yourself.

Fixes

Broken pages, forms that stopped sending, layouts that fall apart on phones, links that go nowhere.

Software updates

Where your platform needs it, we keep themes, plugins and other software up to date, and check the site afterwards.

Backups

We make sure there’s a recent copy of your site, and that you know where it is.

Speed & usability checks

Periodic checks for slow pages, oversized images and anything that makes the site harder to use.

Questions

A straight answer when an email from your host, a renewal notice or a “your website” sales call leaves you unsure.

Outdated systems

Help untangling old setups

If your website, domain and email feel like a pile of accounts nobody remembers setting up, you’re not alone.

We make a simple inventory. Where your domain is registered, who hosts the site, what your email runs on, what renews when, and who has the logins.

We help you get access back. If a previous developer or provider controls your accounts, we’ll help you work through getting them into your name.

We point out what you may not need. Overlapping services and add-ons are common. We’ll explain what each one does so you can decide what to keep.

We move you off systems that are holding you back, when it makes sense, and without breaking things like email along the way.
