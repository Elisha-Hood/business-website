What we help with

The everyday jobs that keep a site useful

  • Content updates. New hours, prices, services or photos. We make the change, or show you how.
  • Fixes. Broken pages, forms that stopped sending, layouts that fall apart on phones.
  • Software updates. Where your platform needs them, followed by a check that the site still works.
  • Backups. A recent copy of your site, and clarity about where it’s kept.
  • Speed & usability checks. Slow pages, oversized images and anything that makes the site harder to use.
  • Straight answers. When a renewal notice or an email from your host leaves you unsure what to do.

Outdated systems

Help untangling old setups

If your website, domain and email feel like a pile of accounts nobody remembers setting up, you’re not alone.

We make a simple inventory. Where your domain is registered, who hosts the site, what your email runs on, what renews when, and who has the logins.

We help you get access back. If a previous developer or provider controls your accounts, we’ll help you work through getting them into your name.

We point out what you may not need. Overlapping services and add-ons are common. We explain what each one does so you can decide what to keep.

We move you off systems that hold you back, when it makes sense, without breaking things like email along the way.

How support works

Support can be ongoing or as needed. Either way, you’ll know how to reach us, what’s covered, and what anything extra would cost before we do it.

Didn’t build it with us? That’s fine.

We start with a short review of your site to see what it runs on and what condition it’s in. Then we tell you honestly whether supporting it makes sense or whether a rebuild would serve you better.

Questions

Common questions

Can you help with just one fix?

Yes. Tell us what’s wrong and we’ll let you know whether we can help, and what it would involve, before starting.

What platforms do you work with?

Tell us what your site runs on and we’ll tell you honestly whether it’s something we can support well. If it isn’t, we’ll say so.

What happens to my accounts if I stop using your support?

Nothing should change for you. We make sure you know what you own and how to get into it, so you can move on whenever you choose.
