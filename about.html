About Foundry Digital

Two people, one studio in Canton, Georgia

Foundry Digital is run by James and Samuel. One of us focuses on understanding your business and shaping the project; the other builds it and the technology behind it. You work directly with both.

Why two roles

Nothing gets lost between the plan and the build

We split the work so nothing falls through the cracks. James makes sure the plan reflects how your business actually works. Samuel makes sure the finished site does what the plan promised.

There’s no account manager in between, so the people you talk to are the people doing the work, and decisions get made with both the business and the technical side in the room.

The team

  • J

    James

    Co-founder · Client Relationships & Creative Direction

    James is usually the first person you’ll speak with. He learns what your business does, who your customers are and what you need the website to accomplish, then helps you decide what matters most for this project. He leads the big-picture thinking and creative direction: how the site should feel, what it should say first and how the pages fit together. Throughout the project he keeps the work tied to your goals and explains decisions in plain terms.

  • S

    Samuel

    Co-founder · Development & Technical Systems

    Samuel leads the coding and development. He turns the agreed plan into a working website: the page layouts, mobile views, forms and the setup that puts it online. When a project needs more than pages, he builds the technical systems behind it, such as the paid-course experience for ProGlide Academy. He also handles the details owners rarely see but rely on, like moving a site without breaking old links and making sure the contact form actually delivers.

How we work together

From your goals to a finished site

  1. James turns your goals into a direction. Priorities, page plan, tone and design direction, agreed with you before the build starts.
  2. Samuel builds it, and anything behind it. Layouts, forms, integrations and launch, checked on common phones, tablets and desktop browsers.
  3. We review it together. Design and build decisions are made side by side, so the finished site feels like one piece rather than two halves.

Where we work

Based in Canton, working across Metro Atlanta

Most of our clients are in Canton, Woodstock, Holly Springs and across Metro Atlanta, and we can meet in person locally.

We also work remotely by video call, phone and email with businesses outside Georgia. Our local focus is where we do most of our work, not a limit on who we work with.
