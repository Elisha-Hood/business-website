Who we help

For good businesses that feel stuck with their website.

Plenty of well-run businesses end up with a site that fell behind, a system nobody explained, or a bill that keeps growing. That isn’t a reflection of your work. Usually it just means you’ve been busy running the business.

01

It’s hard to use on a phone

Your site may have made sense when it was built. Now many visitors arrive on a phone, and small text or fiddly menus get in their way. It’s a common problem, and a fixable one.

02

Simple changes feel hard

Updating your hours, prices or photos shouldn’t mean hunting for a login or waiting weeks. We set things up so updates are straightforward, whether you make them or we do.

03

You’re not sure what you’re paying for

Hosting, plugins, renewals, a developer who stopped answering. We’ll help you work out what you have, what you need, and what you can let go of.

Services

Design, rebuilds and steady support.

Most projects start with a new or rebuilt site and continue with ongoing help. You can choose either one on its own.

Design & redesign

A professional site planned around your business and the questions your customers ask, or a careful rebuild of the one you have.

Mobile-friendly

Readable text, buttons sized for thumbs, and tap-to-call where it helps. We check layouts on common phone, tablet and desktop sizes before launch.

Fast, lean pages

Properly sized images and no unnecessary add-ons, so pages stay quick on ordinary phone connections.

Search basics

Clear page titles, descriptions and structure so search engines can understand your site. We can’t promise rankings, and we’ll be honest about what helps.

Forms & booking

Contact forms, quote requests or booking tools that send to an inbox you choose, tested before launch.

Ongoing support

Updates, fixes and small changes after launch, plus a clear way to ask for them. Also available for sites we didn’t build.

How it works

From first look to launch in four steps.

  1. Free review

    Send us your current site, or tell us you don’t have one. We’ll reply with an honest look at what’s working and what we’d suggest. No obligation.

  2. Scope & plan

    We agree on pages, features, timing and price in writing, and you approve the design direction before we build.

  3. Build & launch

    We build the site, move your content over, and check it on common phones, tablets and desktop browsers before it goes live.

  4. Ongoing help

    After launch we’re here for questions and updates, with ongoing support available if you want it.
