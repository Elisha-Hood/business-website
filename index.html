Why it matters

An old website is quietly costing you customers.

01

They're on their phone

Most people find local businesses on mobile. If they have to pinch and zoom to read your hours, they hit back and call the next result.

02

They judge in seconds

A dated site makes a great business look closed, careless, or out of touch, before anyone reads a word about you.

03

Slow means gone

Heavy pages and giant images make people wait. Every extra second of loading is another chance for them to leave.
