Working with us

How a project runs, from first conversation to launch.

  • Practical recommendations. We suggest what will help customers find and contact you, and say so when something isn’t worth paying for.
  • A scope you can read. Before work starts you get the pages, features, timeline and cost in writing.
  • Direct access. You work with James and Samuel, the two people planning and building your site.
  • Ownership and access. You’ll know what you own, where it lives, and how to get into it.

  1. First conversation

    Your goals come first.

    Tell us about your customers and services, and share your current site if you have one. We reply with what we’d recommend and why.

  2. Scope & cost

    A written plan before any work.

    We agree the pages, features, timeline and cost, and you approve the design direction before we build.

  3. Build & launch

    Built, moved over, and checked.

    Samuel builds the site, we move your content and keep old links working, and we check it on common phones, tablets and desktop browsers before launch.

  4. After launch

    Still reachable after launch.

    We show you how updates work and stay available for questions, with ongoing support agreed separately if you want it.
