What’s included

What a typical project covers

Every business is different, so the exact list is agreed in your written scope. Most projects include:

Planning

A conversation about your customers, your services and what you want the site to do, followed by a simple page plan you approve.

Design

A custom look that fits your business, using your logo, colors and photos. If you don’t have these, we’ll talk through practical options.

Mobile-friendly build

Pages that adapt to phones, tablets and desktops, with readable text, sensible buttons and tap-to-call where it helps.

Contact options

A contact or quote form that sends to an inbox you choose, and booking tools if your business uses them. Tested before launch.

Search basics

Descriptive page titles, clear headings, a sitemap, and guidance on your Google Business Profile. Groundwork, not guaranteed rankings.

Launch & handover

Connecting your domain, final checks, and a walkthrough of how to make simple updates or ask us to make them.

Redesigns

Replacing an older site, carefully

A redesign shouldn’t mean starting from nothing or losing what already works.

We start with what you have. We look at your current pages, the content that’s still accurate, and anything customers already rely on, like a menu, a price list or a booking link.

We keep old links working. Where page addresses change, we set up redirects so bookmarks and search results still lead somewhere useful.

We untangle the setup. Old sites often come with a confusing mix of domain, hosting and email accounts. We’ll help you understand what’s there and avoid breaking anything, like your email, during the move.

For help keeping things running after launch, see maintenance & support.

Questions

Common questions

Do we need to be in the same area?

No. We work remotely by video call, phone and email, so we can help businesses wherever they’re based.

Can you keep my current domain name?

In most cases, yes. We’ll check where your domain is registered and who controls it, then plan the switch so your site and email keep working.

Will a new website get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can honestly promise that. A well-built site with clear, useful content gives search engines a better chance to understand and show your pages, but rankings depend on many things outside a website’s control.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback come together. Your written scope will include an expected timeline, and we’ll tell you if anything changes it.

Who owns the website when it’s done? Proposed

Our intent is that your domain, content and site accounts are in your name, with access you control. The exact terms will be written into your agreement.
