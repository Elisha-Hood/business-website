What’s included

What a typical project covers

The exact list is agreed in your scope before work starts.

  • Planning. A conversation about your customers and services, then a simple page plan you approve.
  • Design. A custom look that fits your business, using your logo, colors and photos.
  • Mobile-friendly build. Readable text, sensible buttons and tap-to-call where it helps.
  • Contact options. A contact or quote form sent to an inbox you choose, tested before launch.
  • Search basics. Descriptive page titles, clear headings, a sitemap, and guidance on your Google Business Profile.
  • Launch & handover. Connecting your domain, final checks, and a walkthrough of how updates work.

Redesigns

Replacing an older site, carefully

A redesign shouldn’t mean starting from nothing or losing what already works.

We start with what you have. Current pages, content that’s still accurate, and anything customers already rely on, like a price list or a booking link.

We keep old links working. Where page addresses change, we set up redirects so bookmarks and search results still lead somewhere useful.

We untangle the setup. Older sites often come with a mix of domain, hosting and email accounts. We map out what’s there so nothing, like your email, breaks during the move.

How pricing works

Every project starts with a free review. We then recommend a scope that fits your needs and budget, and give you a clear price for it before any work starts. If something outside that scope comes up, we’ll talk it through with you before doing extra work.

What we’ll ask from you

Your logo and any photos you’d like to use, details about your services, and access to (or details of) your domain and current site. If you’re not sure where any of that is, that’s fine. Finding it is part of what we help with.

Questions

Common questions

Do we need to be in the same area?

No. We’re based in North Georgia and are happy to meet in person around North Georgia and Metro Atlanta, but we also work by video call, phone and email with businesses anywhere.

Can you keep my current domain name?

In most cases, yes. We’ll check where your domain is registered and who controls it, then plan the switch so your site and email keep working.

Will a new website get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can honestly promise that. A well-built site with clear, useful content gives search engines a better chance to understand and show your pages, but rankings depend on many things outside a website’s control.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback come together. We’ll give you an expected timeline when we agree the scope, and tell you if anything changes it.

Who owns the website when it’s done?

Before work starts, we’ll explain what you’ll own, where everything lives, and how you get into it, so you’re never dependent on us to access your own site.
