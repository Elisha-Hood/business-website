Website design and redesign for small businesses

A website planned around your services and your customers’ questions, designed to look professional and built to work properly on a phone. For new businesses, and for owners whose current site no longer reflects the quality of their work.

Who it’s for

Built for owner-run businesses

Most of our clients run the business themselves and want a website they don’t have to think about every week.

  • Contractors and trades, like painters, remodelers and repair services, who need customers to see real work and call or request an estimate.
  • Local service companies with several services that each deserve a clear explanation of their own.
  • Independent professionals who need a credible site that explains what they do and how to book or get in touch.
  • Owners replacing a site that’s hard to update, awkward on a phone, or tied to accounts nobody can log into.

What you receive

What a project includes

Every project is scoped individually, but most include the following. Anything you don’t need stays out of the scope and the cost.

  • Planning. A page plan built around your services and the questions customers ask before they call, approved before design starts.
  • Design. A custom design using your logo, colors and real photos, with phone layouts designed alongside the desktop ones.
  • Content. Page wording written or edited with you, so each service is explained in plain language.
  • Contact and next steps. Call, text or quote buttons where customers look for them, and a form that only confirms a request once it has been delivered.
  • Search setup. Page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, structured business details where they apply, and help connecting Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile.
  • Launch and handover. Connecting your domain, redirects from old page addresses, checks on common devices, and a record of where your domain, hosting and accounts live.

Redesigns

Replacing a site you already have

We start by deciding what to keep. Sometimes a few fixes are enough; sometimes the site needs rebuilding. We’ll tell you which, and why.

Keep what works. Content that’s still accurate, pages that already bring in visitors, and anything customers rely on, like a price list or a booking link.

Move it carefully. When page addresses change, the old ones redirect to the right new pages. On the Impressive Finishes project, the old FAQ address and a broken homepage link now lead where they should.

Protect your email. Moving a website often means changing domain settings, so we map them first and keep business email working through the switch.

How costs are worked out

Every project is priced from its scope. After a first conversation we recommend what to include and give you a written price before any work starts. The main things that affect cost are the number of pages, how much writing and photography is needed, and any functionality beyond standard contact forms. If something outside the scope comes up, we discuss it before doing extra work.

What we’ll ask from you

Your logo and any photos you’d like to use, details about your services, and access to (or details of) your domain and current site. If you’re not sure where any of that is, that’s fine. Finding it is part of what we help with.

Questions

Common questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the scope: how many pages, how much content needs writing, and whether you need anything beyond standard contact forms. You get a written price for the agreed scope before any work starts.

What’s included in a website project?

Planning, design, phone layouts, contact forms, search setup and launch are part of most projects, and the exact list is in your written scope. Hosting, ongoing maintenance and ongoing search or content work are separate and optional; see maintenance & support.

Who will I be working with?

James and Samuel. James leads planning and creative direction, and Samuel builds the site. There’s no one in between.

Can you fix my current site instead of rebuilding it?

Often, yes. We’ll look at what it runs on and the condition it’s in, then tell you whether fixes or a rebuild would serve you better.

Will a new website get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can honestly promise that. A well-built site with clear, useful content gives search engines a better chance to understand and show your pages, but rankings depend on many things outside a website’s control.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback come together. We’ll give you an expected timeline when we agree the scope, and tell you if anything changes it.

Who owns the website when it’s done?

Before work starts, we’ll explain what you’ll own, where everything lives, and how you get into it, so you’re never dependent on us to access your own site.

Can you keep my current domain name?

In most cases, yes. We’ll check where your domain is registered and who controls it, then plan the switch so your site and email keep working.

Do we need to be in the same area?

No. We’re based in Canton, Georgia and can meet in person around Canton and North Metro Atlanta, but we also work by video call, phone and email with businesses anywhere.
