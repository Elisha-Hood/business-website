Structure

A page for each main service (interior painting, exterior painting, drywall and carpentry repair, and deck and fence staining), plus a services overview, project gallery, service areas, FAQ, about and contact pages.

Design

A deep green and cream palette with a serif accent, suited to an established family business.

Phone usability

Call, text and “free estimate” buttons stay in reach at the bottom of the screen on phones, and the phone number is in the header. A skip-to-content link helps keyboard users.

Getting in touch

An estimate request form that asks for the details a painter needs to quote, plus an FAQ covering warranty, insurance, pricing and preparation.

Concept demo

Riverside Plumbing

The before-and-after slider on our homepage uses a fictional plumbing company. It shows the kind of changes a redesign usually brings: a layout that adapts to phones, a clear headline, services you can scan, and obvious ways to book or call.

It’s a demonstration, not a client project, and it isn’t based on measured results.
