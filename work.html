A website for a family-owned painting company serving North Metro Atlanta. The new site is built and running on a staging address for review. It hasn’t launched on their own domain yet.

Business
House painting and repairs
Scope
New multi-page website
Status
Staging, kept out of search results until launch

Desktop viewScreenshot to be added once Impressive Finishes approves it.

Phone viewPending approval

Structure

Separate pages for each main service (interior painting, exterior painting, drywall and carpentry repair, and deck and fence staining), plus a services overview, project gallery, service areas, an FAQ, an about page and a contact page.

Design

A deep green and cream palette with a serif accent, chosen to suit an established family business.

Phone usability

Call, text and “free estimate” buttons stay in reach at the bottom of the screen on phones, and the phone number is in the header on every page. There’s also a skip-to-content link for keyboard users.

Getting in touch

An estimate request form that asks for the details a painter needs to quote, plus an FAQ covering common questions about warranty, insurance, pricing and preparation.

Before launch: Impressive Finishes’ permission to feature the project and use screenshots, and an update to this description once the site is live. No traffic or lead figures are shown because the site hasn’t launched.

Concept demo

Riverside Plumbing

The before-and-after slider on our homepage uses a fictional plumbing company. It shows the kind of changes a redesign usually involves: a layout that adapts to phones, a clear headline, services you can scan, and obvious ways to book or call.

It’s a demonstration, not a client project, and it isn’t based on measured results.
