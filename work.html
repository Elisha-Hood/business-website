Our work

Real projects, described as they are. We only share results we’ve actually measured, and only with the client’s permission.

Completed · live at impressivefinishes.com

Impressive Finishes, before and after.

A new website for a family-owned painting company serving North Metro Atlanta, now live at impressivefinishes.com (opens in a new tab). The “before” shows the site it replaced.

Organized services

A page for each main service (interior painting, exterior painting, drywall and carpentry repair, and deck and fence staining), plus a services overview, project gallery, service areas, FAQ, about and contact pages.

Clearer first impression

The opening screen now says what the company does and where, with the phone number and a “Get a Free Estimate” button in the header, in a deep green and cream palette suited to an established family business.

Easier on phones

Call, text and “free estimate” buttons stay in reach at the bottom of the screen on phones. A skip-to-content link helps keyboard users.

Simpler estimate requests

An estimate request form that asks for the details a painter needs to quote, plus an FAQ covering warranty, insurance, pricing and preparation.

New website, live on a preview address

ProGlide Academy, built from scratch.

A brand-new website for ProGlide Academy, an FAA Part 107 drone pilot course taught by a working commercial drone pilot. There was no site before, so we started with a blank page and his own aerial footage.

His own footage, front and center

The opening screen plays a clip from the instructor’s drone showreel behind a camera-viewfinder overlay, so visitors see the view from his drone before they read a word.

Proof from real jobs

A “From the field” section with his full showreel, a site-visit time-lapse and a gallery of his aerial photos: night concrete pours, resorts, waterfront homes and top-down construction shots.

A curriculum you can scan

All 20 course modules grouped into five sections that open and close, with counters for the modules, practice exams and flashcard sets, and a “boarding pass” showing what’s included.

Details that fit the subject

A scroll altimeter that climbs to 400 feet (the legal drone ceiling) as you read, and a flight path that draws itself through the steps from enrolling to getting licensed. It’s built to load fast on phones.
